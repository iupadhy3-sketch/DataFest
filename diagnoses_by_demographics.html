Diagnoses by Demographics

What conditions are most prevalent in each group?

Key Findings:
  • All groups: #1 diagnosis is Radiculopathy (back pain)
  • Older patients: More osteoporosis, atrial fibrillation, rheumatoid arthritis
  • Younger patients: More respiratory issues (bronchiolitis, upper respiratory infections)
  • Males: Higher rates of heart disease (atherosclerosis)
  • Females: Higher rates of osteoporosis
  • Black patients: Higher iron deficiency anemia rates
  • Hispanic patients: Higher respiratory infections, iron deficiency
