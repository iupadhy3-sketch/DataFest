American Native Patient Analysis

Analysis of 1,225 American Indian/Alaska Native patients (7,529 encounters)

Key Finding: American Natives actually have shorter gaps between visits than the overall population (7.0 vs 9.0 days median). They also have significantly higher rates of rheumatoid arthritis.
